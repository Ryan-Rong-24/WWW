Danica is a sophomore at Stanford studying Computer Science and Energy Science & Engineering. She has spoken at the United Nations Climate Summit (COP28) and has been featured by outlets including Fox, NBC5, the Chicago Tribune, the NYT, and NPR Chicago. She is passionate about leveraging technology to build solutions that win because they are faster, more innovative, and more efficient—and also tackle global challenges like climate change. This past summer, she was the first hire and engineer at an energy and industrial development startup. She now works with a climate tech VC firm, develops energy optimization and load-balancing algorithms for Stanford Research Computing, and leads Stanford Climate Week.
